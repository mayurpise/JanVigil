Crucial Citizen Rule: Anonymous Tips vs. Bribe Traps

While agencies accept source tips for preliminary inquiry, conducting an immediate official bribe trap requires an identifiable complainant to participate in pre-trap proceedings. Haryana ACB protects informant identity in preliminary inquiries; Andaman & Nicobar Islands strictly disregards unverified address complaints.

🇮🇳 JanVigil.in — Anti-Corruption Reporting & Trap Guidelines

1

DO NOT Pay the Bribe

If a public servant demands an illegal gratification (bribe) for performing an official duty, do not pay or yield to the demand immediately.

2

Contact State ACB / Vigilance Immediately

Call the official Anti-Corruption Bureau toll-free helpline (1064 in most states, 14400 in AP, 1031 in Delhi) or visit the nearest ACB office before the transaction takes place.

3

Pre-Trap Demonstration & Chemical Test

The ACB team registers your complaint, arranges independent pancha witnesses, and applies phenolphthalein powder to currency notes to capture physical proof during hand-over.

4

Trap Execution & On-Spot FIR

The officer is caught red-handed when accepting marked notes, leading to immediate arrest and registration of a Prevention of Corruption Act case.

1. Anonymous Tip

Identity Given: No

Investigation Likelihood: Low to Medium

Used primarily as background intelligence. Most statutory agencies require verifiable identity to initiate formal prosecution.

2. Confidential Complaint

Identity Given: Yes (kept secret by agency)

Investigation Likelihood: High

Identity is recorded by the agency but withheld from the accused public servant to protect the complainant from retaliation.

3. Formal Trap Complaint

Identity Given: Yes

Investigation Likelihood: Very High (Immediate)

Complainant must sign an official FIR statement and assist officers in verifying the bribe demand during pre-trap proceedings.
